# Bill of Materials

| Quantity | Component | Key specification | Purpose |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arduino Mega | Main controller | Main controller |
| 1 | L298N motor-driver module | Drive-motor direction and PWM interface | Controls the rear drive motor |
| 1 | DC drive motor | Kit reference lists 300 RPM | Rear-axle propulsion |
| 1 | Rear differential assembly | Allows different rear-wheel speeds in a turn | Transfers drive to the rear wheels |
| 1 | Steering servo | Servo-operated front steering | Moves the front steering linkage |
| 1 | Ground color sensor | TCS3200 frequency-output interface; S0–S3 and OUT | Detects field color beneath the vehicle |
| 5 | Ultrasonic sensors | Left, front-left, front, front-right and right | Distance-sensing hardware at five orientations |
| 1 | HuskyLens 1 | UART connection through RX1 / TX1 | Onboard vision hardware |
| 1 | Studica battery | 12 V nominal, 3000 mAh | Vehicle power source |
| 1 | Start button | D34 to GND using INPUT_PULLUP | Begins the run |
| 1 | Power switch | Power control | Controls vehicle power |
| 4 | Wheels | Rear: 60 mm diameter; front: 35 mm diameter | Two rear drive wheels and two front steering wheels |
